What's different

Today on woodridgeswimclub.org

  • A Google Calendar in month view, plus off-season and summer hours typed into the page by hand.
  • The two can disagree: the page and the calendar give different end dates for fall lap swim and the winter closure.
  • Renters' practices are not on the calendar, so members can't see who has the pool in the evenings.

In this preview

  • One schedule for the pool, lane by lane: lap swim, lessons, member events and renters together.
  • Every hour on every page is read from it, so the page and the calendar can no longer disagree.
  • Renters keep their own bookings in the same calendar; see the Northwest Water Polo Club preview.
  • Members can follow lap swim from the calendar's filter row and get an email when it changes.
