Pool Rentals and Parties

Full-Pool Rentals to Schools and Private Clubs

Seasonal rentals to schools, swim teams, water polo teams, triathlon training groups, and private swim clubs, in spring, summer, fall or winter, at a fixed hourly rate. Contact the Pool President to discuss.

Ask about a rental

After-hours Pool Rental

Requires a pool membership and booking a week in advance for parties larger than 10. For smaller parties the pool deck gazebo may be reserved with early notice. The pool and deck stay open to members during regular hours.
